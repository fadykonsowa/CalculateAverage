Privacy Policy for Calculate Average

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Calculate Average is a simple mobile application designed to calculate and display the sum and arithmetic average of numbers entered by the user.

Information We Collect

Calculate Average does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or location.

How the Application Works

All calculations are performed locally on the user's device. The numbers entered by the user are used only to calculate and display the sum and arithmetic average.

No entered data is sent to external servers or third parties.

Advertising

Calculate Average does not contain advertisements.

Analytics and Tracking

Calculate Average does not use analytics services, tracking technologies, or advertising identifiers to monitor users.

Third-Party Services

Calculate Average does not use third-party services that collect or process users' personal information.

Children's Privacy

Calculate Average does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if necessary. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
